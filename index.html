Principal's Message

Guiding Today for a Better Tomorrow

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to Maharaja Bir Bikram College, one of the premier institutions of higher education in the state of Tripura. The college is committed to quality education and to an environment that nurtures intellectual growth, creativity and ethical values.

Following the vision of National Education Policy (NEP) 2020, the institution strives to encourage critical thinking, leadership and social responsibility. With a dedicated team of faculty and a vibrant academic atmosphere, the college works to equip students with knowledge and skills for a rapidly changing world.

The college also emphasizes holistic development through co-curricular and extracurricular activities, research initiatives and community engagement. The aim is to inspire lifelong learning and meaningful contribution to society while strengthening academic programmes, infrastructure and innovative teaching methods.

— Principal, Maharaja Bir Bikram College
